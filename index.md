## About Me

I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of California, Santa Cruz, advised by [Dr. Chenguang Wang](https://cgraywang.github.io/), and a visiting researcher at Scale AI. I have worked on evaluation of frontier AI agents, with a special interest in software engineering (SWE) agents on challenging cybersecurity tasks. I am drawn to the question of when agents can be trusted with real systems: how to build evaluations that are realistic and hard to game, how to measure what agents can actually do rather than what they have memorized, and how to turn their failures into better training. I am also fortunate to work with [Prof. Dawn Song](https://dawnsong.io/) at [Berkeley RDI](https://rdi.berkeley.edu/).

Before UCSC, I earned my B.S. in Data Science from Washington University in St. Louis, where I worked on AI for Science at the [Hengen Lab](https://hengenlab.org/) with [Dr. Keith Hengen](https://hengenlab.org/kbh-2) and [Dr. James McGregor](https://hengenlab.org/james-mcgregor).
